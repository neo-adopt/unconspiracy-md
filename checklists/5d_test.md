# The 5D Test

Based on Anirudh Badam's five-D framework in *The AI Unconspiracy*.

Review whether this agent contributes to the disruption the developer or company is participating in, and how that participation benefits them in the short term. The five Ds are **Disruptor, Delivering, Disintermediation, Deflation, and Diversification**.

For each checked item, cite concrete evidence from the project, customer use, or measured results. Mark missing evidence as **Unknown** and explain what would establish it. A planned benefit is not an observed result.

## Review result

Look for evidence of **Disruptor + Delivering + at least one of Disintermediation, Deflation, or Diversification**. Identify the agent's contribution to that combination. A company's success does not automatically establish a benefit to the developer.

- [ ] **Pass** — the combination is supported by evidence, the agent contributes to it, and the developer's or company's short-term benefit is clear.
- [ ] **Revise** — a claimed contribution is unsupported or the agent's connection to the opportunity needs work.
- [ ] **Unknown** — evidence is missing; name the question, experiment, or customer result needed.
- [ ] **Not applicable** — explain why this agent falls outside the test.
