# Only yoU can beat AI

Based on Anirudh Badam's “Only yoU (OU) can beat AI” edition of *The AI Unconspiracy*.

Use this checklist to review what the developer's reading, writing, encounters, and conversations contribute to the agent. Look for original material and judgment grounded in lived experience, and show where they influence the agent's work.

For each checked item, link to the material and an example of how the agent uses it. Mark missing evidence as **Unknown**. The reviewing agent must not invent experiences, opinions, or records to complete the checklist.

## 1. Read more — sources interpreted through experience

- [ ] The agent draws on original human work or material published by an authoritative entity in the developer's field, such as the IRS for tax and accounting.
- [ ] The developer has recorded their own commentary: what they think, what they question, and what the source means for their clients, work, or experience.
- [ ] The source and the developer's interpretation are distinguishable and traceable. Reading AI-generated summaries alone does not count toward this exercise.

## 2. Write more — original material for another human

- [ ] The agent draws on the developer's original writing intended for another person: a memo, email, article, explanation, critique, or client note. Prompts alone do not count.
- [ ] The writing expresses the developer's own judgment, observations, feelings, or point of view.
- [ ] The agent preserves the meaning and context of that contribution, with an output or decision showing its influence.

**Evidence — original writing, intended audience, and its influence:**  

## 3. Meet more — firsthand encounters

- [ ] The agent draws on notes or reflections from actual in-person meetings with clients, colleagues, customers, or professional acquaintances.
- [ ] Those records capture something learned through the encounter: a concern, disagreement, constraint, observation, or changed understanding.
- [ ] The record identifies the encounter and its context where appropriate, and shows how the learning informs the agent's work.

**Evidence — meeting record, firsthand learning, and its use:**  

## 4. Talk more — judgment expressed and tested with people

- [ ] The agent draws on ideas the developer has explained or discussed with other people, in person or in a live meeting. Narrating to AI alone does not count.
- [ ] Notes, whiteboard explanations, transcripts, or follow-up reflections capture the developer's view and the questions or feedback it received.
- [ ] The agent uses the resulting clarification, disagreement, or revised judgment accurately.

**Evidence — discussion, human contribution, feedback, and its use:**  

## Originality check

- [ ] The material presented as the developer's experience or judgment originated with that person. AI-generated opinions and anecdotes do not count as human contributions.
- [ ] AI assistance with transcription, organization, or editing is identified and checked against the original. It does not establish that an encounter or an original thought occurred.
- [ ] Any AI-generated source material accepted under the authoritative-source exception is verified as published by the relevant authority. An AI answer citing an authority does not qualify on that basis alone.
- [ ] The reviewer can point to a specific output or decision shaped by these human contributions and explain what would be missing without them.

## Result

- [ ] **Pass** — traceable human material and judgment meaningfully shape the agent's work; gaps across the four practices are recorded.
- [ ] **Revise** — the material or its effect is generic, misattributed, or unsupported.
- [ ] **Unknown** — evidence is missing; identify the original source, record, or example needed.
- [ ] **Not applicable** — explain why this agent falls outside the test.
